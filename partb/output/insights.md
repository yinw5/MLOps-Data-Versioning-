# Chicago Airbnb, Sept 2025: reporting insights

Data: Inside Airbnb scrape of Chicago (2025-09-22), 7,730 active listings after cleaning (price $10-$2000), 492,465 reviews since 2009.

## Where
- Supply is concentrated: Near North Side, West Town, Near West Side hold 28% of all listings (out of 76 neighbourhoods).
- Priciest areas with 50+ listings: Near North Side, Loop, Near South Side (median $231/night vs $152 citywide).

## What
- 80% of listings are entire homes/apts, at a median $177/night vs $60 for a private room.
- Ratings are high almost everywhere: median 4.87/5; superhosts 4.91 vs others 4.77.

## Who
- 69% of listings belong to hosts with 2+ listings; operators with 10+ listings alone run 35% of the market.
- Single-listing hosts rate higher (median 4.91 vs 4.83) and are superhosts more often (49% vs 39%).

## Rules
- 34% of listings require 30+ night stays. Only 6% of those show a city license, vs 99.5% of short-term listings. Chicago doesn't require registration for 30+ day rentals, so many hosts likely set the minimum stay to avoid it.

## When
- Reviews fell 50% in 2020 (COVID), recovered past 2019 by 2021, and peaked at 102,167 in 2024.
- Strong seasonality (2022-2025 average): August is the busiest month (10,329 reviews), February the quietest (3,619), about 2.9x apart.
